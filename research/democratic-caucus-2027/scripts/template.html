What the sort shows

    Ideology map

    Left score against centrist score

    Each dot is one member. Across: Centrism Index (CI). Up: Left-Ideology Index (LI). Each panel highlights one group against the rest in grey. The darker guide lines mark the thresholds (LI 6 for the left group, CI 5 for the centrist group). Groups 2 and 3 are separated by experience and engagement, not by this map. Filled dots meet the group's test outright; rings are nearest-fit placements. Hover or focus a dot for the name; select it to open the record.

    Roster

    Every member, with the evidence

    Select a row to see the positions found, the sources, the debate and media record, and the reasoning. Scores: LI (0–16), CI (0–10), EPD experience & policy depth (0–7), EO engagement & openness (0–5). Small-$ is the share of individual donations under $200 (FEC, to 14 Oct 2026).
    Method

    How the test works

    Each member was researched against the same checklist: campaign issue pages, primary-season questionnaires and coverage, endorsements, debate and interview records, prior office, and FEC filings. A position counts only with an explicit public statement, pledge, vote or endorsement, and each one is cited. Missing evidence scores zero. The scores are then run through a fixed decision sequence.
